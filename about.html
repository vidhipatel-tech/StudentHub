About the portal

ABOUT

StudentHub is a focused digital space for students to access campus information, events and student services.

What is StudentHub?

StudentHub connects important student information in one place. Students can view events, maintain their profile, access their dashboard and communicate through the portal.

Purpose

The portal provides a clear student-facing interface for campus activities, course information, notices, FAQs, feedback and contact services.

Student Services

  • Student dashboard and profile
  • Campus events and registration
  • Course and semester information
  • FAQs, feedback and contact

Portal Access

The Hub menu provides separate Student and Admin dashboard entries so each area can be opened directly.
